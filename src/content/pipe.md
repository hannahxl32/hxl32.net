# Particle Imager for Plankton in the Environment

The PIPE is an almost fully 3D-printable pumped fluid microscope targeting particles in the range of 10-500 micron. As designed, it requires ***zero*** specialist components, with everything being either 3D-printable, readily avalaible to purchase on sites like eBay, or quickly made with a hacksaw and drill.

![View of the printable parts of PIPE in Blender](/static/content/pipe/model_view.jpg)

The latest version can resolve details down to about 2 micron (limited by optical resolution).

![Image of Saccharomyces cerevisiae](/static/content/pipe/yeast_2026-11-12_19-40.jpg)

Above is an image of Saccharomyces cerevisiae, or brewer's yeast, taken with the PIPE. No filters have been applied, and each cell is about 7 micron in diameter. 

PIPE is open hardware under the CERN OHL-S licence, and you can download the plans to build your own at [https://github.com/habaldwin01/pipe](https://github.com/habaldwin01/pipe).
